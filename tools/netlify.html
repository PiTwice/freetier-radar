Netlify free tier: what you actually get

Hosting & PaaS No card required Verified 2026-08-09

Free plan is now credit-based: 300 credits/month to spend on deploys, bandwidth and compute.

The limits, verified

  • 300 credits / mo
  • ≈ 15 GB bandwidth (20 credits/GB) or ~20 production deploys (15 credits each)
  • 1 team member

Watch out — the fine print

  • Sites STOP SERVING traffic when credits run out (until next cycle)
  • Frequent production deploys burn credits fast

Should you build on it?

Netlify can absolutely be used for free, but go in with eyes open: the 2 caveats above are the exact things that catch people in production. If your project can tolerate them, it's a fine pick; if not, check the related free tiers below for an alternative.
